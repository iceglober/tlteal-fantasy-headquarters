How the score works

Each team gets a 0–100 power score from four inputs. Each input is scaled so the best team in the league gets 1 and the worst gets 0.

  • All-play record — your record if you played every team every week. Removes schedule luck.
  • Points per game — raw scoring.
  • Actual win % — the standings still matter.
  • Roster strength — your best possible lineup's projected points this week, using Sleeper projections run through this league's scoring rules. Players on bye fall back to their season-long per-game projection.

Early in the season results are noisy, so roster strength carries most of the weight. Each game played shifts weight toward results: roster weight is 75% − 7% × games played, never below 25%. The rest splits 45% all-play, 35% points per game, 20% win %.

Luck = actual wins − expected wins from all-play. Positive means your schedule has helped you. Click any row to see the projected lineup.
